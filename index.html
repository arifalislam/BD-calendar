Sunrise and sunset time in Bangladesh

BD Calendar lists the sunrise and sunset time for every day of the previous, current and next year. Times are calculated for Dhaka (23.81° N, 90.41° E, UTC+6) and shown in 12-hour format. Switch between Day, Week and Month views, and select a date to see its Bengali and Hijri date.

The six seasons of Bangladesh (ছয় ঋতু)

Bangladesh counts six ritu, each two Bengali months long, starting with Grishmo at Pohela Boishakh on 14 April.

  • গ্রীষ্ম Grishmo · Summer

    Boishakh – Joishtho · 14 Apr – 14 Jun

    The hot season. Afternoons are hot and humid, and sudden nor’westers (kalbaishakhi) bring storms. Mango, jackfruit and litchi ripen.

  • বর্ষা Barsha · Monsoon

    Asharh – Srabon · 15 Jun – 15 Aug

    The monsoon. Heavy rain fills the rivers and floods low fields. This is the main rainfall season.

  • শরৎ Sharat · Autumn

    Bhadro – Ashwin · 16 Aug – 15 Oct

    Rain eases, skies clear and kash flowers bloom along the riverbanks. Durga Puja falls in this season.

  • হেমন্ত Hemanto · Late autumn

    Kartik – Ogrohayon · 16 Oct – 14 Dec

    Mornings turn cool and misty and the aman rice harvest begins. Nabanna, the new-rice festival, is held in Ogrohayon.

  • শীত Sheet · Winter

    Poush – Magh · 15 Dec – 12 Feb

    The coolest and driest months, with fog in the north. It is the season of khejur ras (date palm sap) and pithas.

  • বসন্ত Bosonto · Spring

    Falgun – Choitro · 13 Feb – 13 Apr

    Krishnachura and polash trees flower. Pohela Falgun, on 13 February, welcomes the season.

Bengali and Hijri dates

The Bengali date follows the revised calendar used in Bangladesh: five months of 31 days, seven of 30, and one extra day in Falgun in leap years. The Hijri date is a lunar calendar calculated with the Umm al-Qura method. Bangladesh sights the moon locally, so the date popup lets you shift the Hijri date by one day either way.

Frequently asked questions

What time are sunrise and sunset in Dhaka?

Through the year the sun rises in Dhaka between about 5:11 am and 6:43 am and sets between about 5:11 pm and 6:50 pm. Open the Day view to see today's exact sunrise and sunset.

How long are the longest and shortest days in Bangladesh?

In Dhaka the longest day, around 21 June, has about 13 hours 36 minutes of daylight. The shortest, around 22 December, has about 10 hours 40 minutes.

What are the six seasons of Bangladesh?

Bangladesh follows six ritu (seasons) of two Bengali months each: Grishmo (summer), Barsha (monsoon), Sharat (autumn), Hemanto (late autumn), Sheet (winter) and Bosonto (spring).

When is Pohela Boishakh, the Bengali New Year?

In Bangladesh's revised Bengali calendar, Pohela Boishakh always falls on 14 April. From that day the Bengali year is the Gregorian year minus 593.

Why can the Hijri date differ by a day?

The Hijri date here is calculated with the Umm al-Qura method. Bangladesh sights the new moon locally, so the official date can be one day later. Use the shift control in the date popup to match your local date.

Are these times exact for my district?

Times are calculated for Dhaka (23.81° N, 90.41° E). Places east of Dhaka see the sun a few minutes earlier and places west see it a few minutes later, by up to about 8 minutes across the country.

Sunrise and sunset use the NOAA solar algorithm for Dhaka, rounded to the minute. Other districts differ by a few minutes. Seasons follow the Bengali months, two to a season. Bengali dates use Bangladesh's revised calendar. Hijri dates are calculated (Umm al-Qura); Bangladesh's own moon sighting can land a day apart, so the date popup lets you shift it.
